## 1. `Elitism in Gaetano Mosca_s Treatment.pdf.txt`

**Ідентифікація.** Автори: Garineh Keshishyan та Reza vahabi (PhD Student). Назва: «Elitism in Gaetano Mosca's Treatment». Рік у тексті не вказано; найпізніша позиція в списку літератури — 2010 (Nash, «Contemporary political sociology»), отже праця не раніша за 2010 р. Ключові слова: «Elite, Elitism, Gaetano Mosca».

**Тема 1 (контекст другої половини XIX ст.) — найповніше з усіх семи файлів.**
- Зародження елітизму як школи: «elitism as an intellectual school in political science was first established in 19th century by Mosca and Pareto and gradually spread afterwards. Actually in late 19th century, the mindset led to a new school primarily known as elitism and the thinkers thereof as neo-Machiavellists».
- Песимізм щодо принципів модерну після Першої світової: «Trusting and believing in human reason and interpreting their life based on evolutionism and development, are one of axioms of all the thinkers ever since Renaissance»; «following the bloody word war 1st, this trust in the role of human in history and society was damaged».
- Еволюціонізм: «Evolutionism is another principle of elitism»; «Auguste Comte states the path of social evolution started out from religious thoughts through philosophic ones to practical ideas».
- Марксизм як виклик: «The theorists are anti-Marxists, mainly aiming to reject economic determinist ideas as well as Marx's class struggle. They are also anti-democracy».
- Утопія: «In their view, democracy and socialism are delusions and never fulfilled»; «The theory of elitism was in response to progressive Marxism»; «every revolution claiming to put an end to the elites would eventually end up replacing the former elite with new ones».

**Тема 3 (біографія).** «Gaetano Mosca (1858-1941), Italian political science was born in Sicily, Palermo. He graduated in law field in 1881»; «Mosca studied in university of Palermo and taught constitutional law in the same university (1885-1888), as well as Rome (1888-1896), and Turin (1896-1908) universities. He began his political activity as a member of Italian senate in 1908»; «His last lecture was a critique against Benito Mussolini»; «He was against Nazi racial elitism and disapproved of Marxism so strongly that even he despised Karl Marx and did not believe in democracy». Сицилійський чинник: «It is difficult to understand to what extent the climate of the Sicily, where Mosca lived, impacted these theory. Sicily was both socially and politically the least developed region in Italy»; «thinkers like Antonio Gramsci, Italian Marxist and William Solomon, American historian, attributed Mosca's hatred of democracy to him being a Sicilian». Рісорджименто, trasformismo, парламентська корупція — **у джерелі не висвітлено**.

**Тема 4 (метод).** Побіжно: «Employing historical approaches, entities, and political theories, he put forward the theory of ruling class». Універсальність: «Mosca deem the existence of elite and the masses as universal in all of the political societies». Статистика, «scienza politica» як термін — **у джерелі не висвітлено**.

**Тема 5 (правлячий клас, політична формула, Парето/Міхельс).**
- «Mosca remarked there are two classes of people: the ruling class and the class being ruled, in every society ranging from the less developed ones by far through to those at the dawn of civilization to the most advanced societies».
- Організаційне, не психологічне пояснення: «he also attributed the governance and dominance of an elite minority over the masses to the features of organization rather than superior psychological features. Therefore in his perspective, the dominance of the organized minority with common motivations over the unorganized majority was unavoidable»; «it is impossible for a social order to exist without a political class».
- Політична формула: «every ruling class to protect and legitimize their governances, began to create a political formula»; «'Political formula' does not represent rightfulness or righteousness but it may be merely a myth»; «Mosca's conceptualization of political formula is remarkably similar to the concept of domination».
- Відмінність від Парето: «Pareto stressed on psychological determinism, while Mosca emphasized the role of social organizations»; «While Pareto speaks of elite group, Mosca believes when groups are intellectually unified and connected in terms of family, beneficial, and cultural relations, they turn from groups to classes».
- Міхельс: «every organization is run by a minority group whose opinions are regarded as rules»; Мілле/Бернгам — еліти як «a major threat against democracy».

**Тема 6.** Окремий розділ «Impact of elitism-based notions on political science»; «Nowadays elitism is one of the common patterns in political science and sociology»; «The most important problem for elitist theorists was "who governs", or "who exclusively owns the power?"».

**Тема 2 — у джерелі не висвітлено.**

## 2. `стаття_Mosca_and_the_Theory_of_Elitism_by_Ettore_A_Albertoni.txt`

Albertoni — «a distinguished scholar who teaches at the University of Milan»; книжка — переклад його італійської праці *Dottrina della classe politica e teoria delle elites* (1985).

**Тема 3.** «The originator of this type of theory is Gaetano Mosca (1858-1941), best known as the author of Elementi di scienza politica, which was translated into English as The Ruling Class»; «for most of his life, Mosca was a university professor (at such places as Turin, Milan, and Rome) and a government official (including senator for the last twenty years of his life)». Про термін: «the term elitism is misleading by conveying an anti-democratic connotation which is not necessarily part of the theory; further, we do not really have a "school" in the full-blown sense that sociologists of knowledge deal with».

**Тема 4 (метод) — ключове негативне свідчення.** «there is no sustained discussion of Mosca's method of thinking or the approach he follows in his inquiries, aside from a few scattered remarks about positivism, realism, and his Machiavellian, empirical, and historical approach». Finocchiaro наголошує, що такий аналіз «is always important in the study of a great thinker since it enables us to learn and appreciate important lessons, even while disagreeing about specific substantive details».

**Тема 5.** «Albertoni's account of Mosca focuses on his doctrine of the political class, his attitude toward parliamentarism and democracy, the interplay between the scientific and the ideological components of his thought, and the connection between his thought and Italian historical conditions». Періодизація: ранній Моска «was unable to understand the dialectical relationship between the classes which was taking shape during his formative years» (30); середній — концепція політичного класу «is linked dialectically to the "new concept" toward which his entire research is directed: that of juridical defence» (51); зрілий починається з 2-го видання (1923) *Elementi*, що містить «a new up-to-date and dialectical section» (85), «dialectics in the doctrine of the political class-science, ideology and ethics» (94). Місце в школі: «The elite school seems to have flourished in Italy more than elsewhere, the best known of these authors being Vilfredo Pareto and Robert Michels» (+ Шумпетер 1942, Ч. Р. Міллз 1956, Боттомор 1964). Елітизм і марксизм: обидва «stress class division»; Альбертоні слідує «the traditional interpretation of Gramsci as belonging to the opposite tradition of Marxism».

**Тема 6.** «Such methodological understanding … would have enabled us to understand better the similarities and differences among the various "elitist" thinkers».

## 3. `Introduction. Elite Theory Philosophical Challenges.pdf.txt`

**Ідентифікація.** Giovanni Damele, Andre Santos Campos (Instituto de Filosofia da Nova / IFILNOVA, NOVA FCSH, Lisbon). «Introduction. Elite Theory: Philosophical Challenges», *Topoi* (2022) 41:1–5, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11245-022-09794-1, опубліковано онлайн 10 лютого 2022. Вступна стаття до спецвипуску *Topoi*.

**Тема 1.**
- «a philosophical tradition dating back to the nineteenth century's final decade that sought to identify the internal dynamics of the "elites" or "ruling classes"».
- Поштовх: «Confronted with profound changes in the political landscape due to the progressive enlargement of the franchise in Western democracies, authors such as Vilfredo Pareto, Gaetano Mosca, and Robert Michels insisted on the inevitable oligarchic nature of any kind of collective organisation, including modern mass parties».
- «It is possible to interpret elite theory as a periodical reaction to social revolution. The strong trend in elite theory during the second half of the nineteenth century can be seen as a reaction against socialism».
- Позитивізм: «Strongly influenced by political realism and, in a broad sense, positivism, both Mosca and Pareto sought to establish political science and sociology as objective sciences that made use of empirical methods».
- Епістемологічний реалізм: «Pareto and Mosca, despite their deference to a positivist epistemology, significantly anticipated a sort of epistemological realism unsympathetic to linear notions of causality embedded in contemporary social sciences. With their emphasis on history, contexts and agents, they ushered into the debate of their time some arguments that realist epistemology fully developed».

**Тема 4.**
- «The search for "constants" and "general laws" of political action seemed a natural consequence of such methods. What Mosca called "the struggle for pre-eminence" (Mosca 1939, p. 29) is nothing more than the striving for social and political power seen as a "constant" and a "fundamental law" of every political phenomenon».
- Фасад vs мотиви: «there is a clear distinction between the "façade legitimation", represented by political programs, ideologies, public declarations, and even laws and constitutions, and the real motives for political action. Legitimation may not necessarily be strategical or even conscious».
- Завдання науки: «the object of a "true science of politics", that is, to "understand how the 'political class' recruits itself, maintains itself in power, and legitimates itself through ideologies" (Hirschman 1991, p. 52)».

**Тема 5.** Узагальнення Бернгама (1943: 165 ff.), зручне для курсової: (1) «The primary object of every elite or ruling class is to preserve power and privilege»; (2) «The rule of the elite is based upon (not-necessarily explicit) force and fraud»; (3) «The social structure is sustained by a political formula that typically correlates with a generally accepted religion, ideology or myth»; (4) аристократична та демократична тенденції; (5) «In the long run, the democratic tendency always prevails. Consequently, no social structure is permanent, and no stable utopia is possible»; (6) панування аристократичної тенденції → «rapid shifts … (e.g., social revolutions)». Далі: «no social groups are governed by a majority; all social groups, including societies qualified as democratic, are ruled by a minority»; «the ability of an organised minority to force "its will upon the disorganised majority"». Міхельс і «iron law of oligarchy»: «political parties, trade unions, and other mass organisations are invariably ruled by largely self-serving and self-perpetuating oligarchies». Демократія як свобода: «By liberty, Mosca has in mind "juridical defence" … "a measure of security for the individual which protects him from the arbitrary and irresponsible of personally held power"»; право опозиції — «the right of opponents of the currently governing élite to express publicly their oppositions views and to organise to implement those views». Гіршман: елітисти як приклад «rhetoric of reaction», «futility thesis»: будь-яке суспільство завжди поділене на еліту й не-еліту — теза, «tailor-made [by classical elitists] to prove the futility of any move toward true "political citizenship" via the franchise» (Hirschman 1991, p. 51).

**Тема 3 (Рісорджименто/Італія).** Майже немає. Лише: «Mosca was a conservative whose theories aimed at falsifying the belief in universal suffrage as a "government of the majority"». Сицилія, trasformismo, корупція — **у джерелі не висвітлено**.

**Тема 6.** «Not only did they shape the contemporary approach to the study of the "ruling classes decisively", but they also provided the groundwork for elitist theories of democracy»; «Democratic elitism played a prominent role in explaining the "third wave" of democratisation».

**Тема 2 — частково** (політологія як об'єктивна наука; про відокремлення від моральної філософії та юриспруденції прямо не сказано).
