1. A defining moment in life can reveal who your true friends are. 
2. The speaker states that friends are generally more reliable than family. 
3. The speaker describes a stage in life where they preferred to spend time with 
friends over family. 
4. The speaker believes parents from "another time" are easy to understand due 
to shared experiences. 
5. The speaker's first defining moment occurred during their junior year of high 
school. 
6. Ewing Sarcoma is a type of bone cancer. 
7. During the first illness, all of the speaker's friends abandoned them. 
8. The speaker found it harder to deal with the physical aspects of 
chemotherapy than the social challenges. 
9. The speaker made a very special friend after recovering from the first illness, 
with whom they planned a future. 
10. The speaker was diagnosed with leukemia the night after spending time 
with this very special friend. 
11. The special friend promised to stay by the speaker's side through the second 
illness. 
12. The special friend remained constant and supportive throughout the 
speaker's leukemia treatment. 
13. The speaker realized that friends are always constant and reliable. 
14. The speaker's younger sister's comment made the speaker realize they had 
shut out their family. 
15. At the end of the narrative, the speaker no longer has Ewing Sarcoma or 
leukemia, but still has all of their old friends.
